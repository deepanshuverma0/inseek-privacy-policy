Child Safety Standards

InSeek — Find your people. We are committed to providing a safer environment for people to discover others, build connections, and communicate. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

These standards apply to all users and all features of InSeek, including profiles, photos, connection requests, and private conversations.

1. Prohibition of child sexual abuse and exploitation

InSeek strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This includes:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or distributing CSAM.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to sexually exploit a minor.
  • Sexualizing children or sharing sexualized depictions of minors.
  • Threatening, coercing, sextorting, or trafficking a child.
  • Using InSeek to arrange or facilitate child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Users must not use InSeek to engage in, encourage, promote, or facilitate any form of child abuse or exploitation.

2. Reporting child safety concerns

Anyone who encounters suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or other child safety concerns connected to InSeek should contact our designated safety contact.

Email: support@inseek.co.in

Please include a description of the concern and relevant account details, if available. Do not download, copy, or forward suspected child sexual abuse material. Never attach such material to an email.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services or law enforcement authorities.

3. Review and enforcement

InSeek takes reports of suspected child sexual abuse and exploitation seriously. We will assess reports, take appropriate action consistent with our policies and applicable law, and cooperate with lawful investigations.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, action may include:

  • Removing prohibited content when identified.
  • Restricting, suspending, or terminating accounts involved in abuse.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally appropriate.
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM or suspected exploitation to the appropriate authorities as required by applicable law.

4. Compliance with child safety laws

InSeek is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations. We will respond appropriately to child safety concerns and cooperate with relevant authorities in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

5. Our commitment

We do not permit InSeek to be used for child sexual abuse or exploitation. We are committed to reviewing our safety practices and addressing credible reports in accordance with these standards and applicable law.

6. Child safety contact

For child sexual abuse material (CSAM) prevention, child safety concerns, or questions about these standards, contact:

InSeek Child Safety Contact
Email: support@inseek.co.in

Please use the subject line Child Safety Concern so your message can be identified appropriately.
