Privacy Policy

This policy explains how InSeek handles information when you use our application and its people-discovery, profile, connection, and messaging features.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

InSeek (“InSeek,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) helps users discover and connect with people based on their interests and intentions. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use InSeek.

Where consent is legally required, we will seek it separately.

2. Information We Collect

The information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant. It may include:

  • Account and authentication information: email address, account identifier, name, authentication/session records, and information used to sign in with Google or email verification, depending on your sign-in method.
  • Profile information: details you choose to add, such as your name, profile photo, bio, selected interests, and intentions.
  • Connections and messages: connection requests, connection status, messages you send or receive, reply references, and related delivery or read-status information where supported.
  • Notification information: push-notification tokens and notification-related records needed to deliver or manage notifications.
  • Technical and security information: information made available through the app, hosting, authentication, and infrastructure services to maintain reliability, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot issues. The exact information depends on the services and configuration in use.

We do not intend to collect information that a feature does not need. Please avoid adding sensitive personal information to your profile or messages.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and secure accounts; authenticate sign-ins; display profiles and support discovery based on selected interests and intentions; enable connection requests and messaging; deliver notifications; diagnose errors; prevent abuse; respond to support requests; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

If the app adds analytics, personalized recommendations, advertising, or other data-driven features, this section should be updated to reflect those uses before launch.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

Some sharing is necessary to operate a social discovery and messaging service.

  • Other users: profile information and content may be shown to other users through InSeek features.
  • Service providers: third-party providers may process information on our behalf for authentication, database and backend hosting, image storage, email delivery, and push notifications.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.
  • Business changes: if the service is involved in a merger, acquisition, or transfer, information may be handled as part of that transaction subject to applicable protections and law.

Actual providers, settings, and data flows should be verified against the production app before this policy is published.

5. Profiles, Connections, and Messages

InSeek is designed to help people discover and connect with one another. Information you add to your profile may be visible to other users when displayed through discovery or other app features.

Connection requests and connection status are processed to support connecting users. Messages are processed and stored as needed to provide conversations. People you communicate with may retain information they have already seen, copied, or captured outside the app.

Do not send information through your profile or messages that you would not want another user to see. Update this section if the app's visibility rules change.

6. Security and Data Retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by information type and system.

Verify relevant backup, log, message, image, and provider retention periods so this section accurately reflects actual practice.

7. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You are responsible for keeping your profile information appropriate and up to date. Where the app provides controls, you can manage the information you choose to include in your profile and the permissions you grant on your device.

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information through the account-deletion option in the app, if available, or by contacting us using the details below. We may need to verify your request before processing it.

When an account is deleted, we intend to delete or de-identify associated personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate security needs. Some information may remain for a limited period in backups or records that must be retained by law. The exact deletion outcome depends on the production implementation and provider systems.

Before publishing: test account deletion and ensure the external deletion-request method works. Do not promise deletion from all systems unless that has been verified.

8. Children's Privacy

InSeek is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Confirm that this age statement matches InSeek's intended audience and applicable requirements in the regions where the app is available.

9. International Processing and Your Rights

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. The locations involved depend on the providers and their configurations.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to make a request. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

Verify where production data is stored and whether additional regional disclosures or mechanisms are required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as InSeek's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
