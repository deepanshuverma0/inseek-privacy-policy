How to delete your InSeek account

You can permanently delete your InSeek account directly from the InSeek app.

  1. Open the InSeek app and sign in to your account.
  2. Navigate to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm account deletion.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once your request is processed, your account and associated data will be deleted and you may no longer be able to recover them.

Can't access your account?

If you cannot sign in or are unable to delete your account through the app, contact us at support@inseek.co.in with the subject line InSeek Account Deletion Request. We may ask you to verify ownership of your account before processing your request.
