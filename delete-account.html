Account management

Delete your
InSeek account.

You’re in control of your account. You can permanently delete your InSeek account directly from the app by following the steps below.

How it works

Complete these steps in the InSeek mobile app.

01

Open InSeek

Launch the app and sign in to the account you want to delete.

02

Go to Settings

Open the Settings section in your account.

03

Select Delete Account

Choose the Delete Account option in Settings.

04

Confirm your decision

Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm permanent deletion.

!
This action is permanent

Account deletion cannot be undone. Your account and associated data will be deleted according to InSeek’s data deletion process. Some data may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Need help?

Can't access your account or complete the deletion process? Contact our support team. We may ask you to verify ownership of your account before processing your request.
