Privacy Policy

This policy explains how InSeek handles information when you use our application and its people-discovery, profile, connection, and messaging features.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

On this page

1. Introduction 2. Information we collect 3. How we use information 4. Sharing and providers 5. Profiles and messages 6. Security and retention 7. Choices and deletion 8. Children's privacy 9. International processing 10. Policy changes 11. Contact

1. Introduction

InSeek (“InSeek,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) helps users discover and connect with people based on their interests and intentions. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use InSeek.

Where consent is legally required, we will seek it separately.

2. Information We Collect

The information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant. It may include:

  • Account and authentication information: email address, account identifier, name, authentication or session records, and information used to sign in with Google or email verification.
  • Profile information: details you choose to add, such as your name, profile photo, bio, interests, and intentions.
  • Connections and messages: connection requests, connection status, messages you send or receive, reply references, and supported delivery or read-status information.
  • Notification information: push-notification tokens and notification-related records used to deliver or manage notifications.
  • Technical and security information: information made available through the app, hosting, authentication, and infrastructure services to maintain reliability, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot issues.

Please avoid adding sensitive personal information to your profile or messages.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and secure accounts, authenticate sign-ins, display profiles, support discovery based on selected interests and intentions, enable connection requests and messaging, deliver notifications, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

Some sharing is necessary to operate a social discovery and messaging service.

  • Other users: profile information and content may be shown to other users through InSeek features.
  • Service providers: third-party providers may process information on our behalf for authentication, database and backend hosting, image storage, email delivery, and push notifications, where configured.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to address fraud, abuse, or security threats.
  • Business changes: information may be handled as part of a merger, acquisition, or transfer, subject to applicable protections and law.

Our actual providers and data practices may change as the service develops. This policy should reflect the production app and its current configuration.

5. Profiles, Connections, and Messages

InSeek helps people discover and connect with one another. Information you add to your profile may be visible to other users through discovery or other app features.

Connection requests and connection status are processed to support connecting users. Messages are processed and stored as needed to provide conversations. People you communicate with may retain information they have already seen, copied, or captured outside the app.

Do not share information through your profile or messages that you would not want another user to see.

6. Security and Data Retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by information type and system.

7. Your Choices and Account Deletion

Where the app provides controls, you can manage the information you choose to include in your profile and the permissions you grant on your device.

You can request account deletion using the account-deletion option in the app or through our account deletion page. You may also contact us for assistance.

When an account is deleted, we intend to delete or de-identify associated personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate security needs. Some information may remain for a limited period in backups or records that must be retained by law.

8. Children's Privacy

InSeek is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Read our Child Safety Standards for more information about our child-safety policies.

9. International Processing and Your Rights

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. The locations involved depend on the providers and their configurations.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to make a request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as InSeek's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. We will publish revisions on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or privacy and data-deletion requests, contact:
